How predicted points work

The GW columns estimate each player's FPL points per fixture from the 2026/27 scoring rules, and add them up for a double gameweek (a blank scores 0). Every part is an expected value, so a 30% chance of a 4-point clean sheet counts as 1.2.

  • Minutes and availability. How often the player played, and played 60+ minutes, in the last 4 gameweeks, and their average minutes when they did. FPL's injury flag scales this down (a 75% doubt counts 75%); injured, suspended and unavailable players score 0.
  • Appearance. 1 point for playing, 1 more for 60+ minutes.
  • Goals and assists. Season xG and xA per 90 × expected minutes × the goal value (GK 10, DEF 6, MID 5, FWD 4) or 3 per assist, scaled up against leaky defences and down against tight ones.
  • Clean sheets and goals conceded. The goals the player's team should concede come from the opponent's attack strength. The chance of keeping it at 0 is worth 4 points for GK/DEF and 1 for MID (with 60+ minutes); GK/DEF also lose 1 per 2 expected goals conceded.
  • Saves. Goalkeepers' saves per 90, scaled by the opponent's attack, at 1 point per 3.
  • Defensive contributions. The chance of reaching 10 actions (DEF) or 12 (MID/FWD) from the player's rate per 90, worth 2 points.
  • Bonus and cards. Season bonus per 90, minus 1 per expected yellow and 3 per expected red.

Team strength is each team's xG for and against per match this season, blended with 3 league-average matches so early weeks don't swing too far. Home teams get a 10% boost in both. Per-90 rates are blended with the position average over 90 minutes, so a player with a single cameo isn't projected like a star. Penalties, own goals and set-piece duties aren't modelled separately (they're inside xG). Predictions refresh every Tuesday with the data; the code is in scripts/predict.py.
